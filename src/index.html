Interests

My interests are all real-world problem- and solution-based. My passions involve physics for understanding the natural world, biology for conservation and health, computational tasks to tackle complex problems, and how human relationships shape the challenges we face. Much of my free time is spent learning more about these topics and staying up to date on scientific advancements.

I spend much of my time exploring physics beyond the scope of my course, building a broader understanding of the physical world and the mathematical frameworks we use to describe it. I am strongly interested in the relationship between theoretical understanding and the computational methods we use to explore it, and how numerical techniques are used to investigate complicated systems that would be difficult to understand otherwise.

I'm also very interested in machine learning, particularly its application to scientific research and computational physics. The prospect of using data-driven methods to complement traditional physical modelling is especially appealing to me as a means of identifying complex patterns.

Outside of the sciences, I enjoy learning about history, geopolitics and international relations. I am particularly interested in how historical events, political institutions, geography and economic interests shape the relationships between states and influence international order. I enjoy exploring the historical context behind conflicts, as well as the strategic calculations that influence foreign policy.

Contrary to popular belief, I do spend time outside. I enjoy hiking, camping and adventure activities. My hobbies have led me to join Yorkshire Universities Air Squadron (YUAS), where I have the opportunity to achieve my long-standing ambition of flying, under the instruction of RAF pilots. I enjoy playing hockey for the university, weight training and running.
